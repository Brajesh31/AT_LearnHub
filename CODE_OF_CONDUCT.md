# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We want AT LearnHub to be a respectful, inclusive, and useful learning space. Learners have different experience levels and backgrounds. Questions and mistakes are part of learning.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Explain corrections and provide reproducible examples.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Do not share personal data, credentials, or private course material without permission.
- Credit contributors and sources accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, plagiarism, deliberate disclosure of secrets, or misuse of external services is not acceptable.

## Reporting
Contact the maintainer privately through <https://github.com/brajesh31>. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy.
